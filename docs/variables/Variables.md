Variables and commands can be used to change almost everything about how the game works. Type them in the console, such as `/fov 100`, or put them in your `autoexec.cfg`. These pages are made from the descriptions in [`config/usage.cfg`](https://github.com/redeclipse/base/blob/master/config/usage.cfg), and the game's Docs reader makes its own from the version you're playing.

Where a family of variables shares a pattern, such as one for each weapon, the pages list it once with a placeholder (such as `<weapon>` or `<team>`), and the values it stands for are given above the table.

- [General](General): Joining and leaving games, your player, recording movies and demos, and the clients in a game
- [Movement](Movement): How players move: speeds, jumping and the other impulse moves, gravity, and moving through liquids
- [Gameplay](Gameplay): The rules of a match: health, damage, spawning, items, teams and scoring
- [Server](Server): Running a server: its settings, the privileges needed for each action, moderation and the IRC relay
- [Modes](Modes): Settings for each mode, and the aliases that pick a mode and its mutators
- [Sound](Sound): Volume, music, footsteps, and how sounds carry, echo and give way to the announcer
- [Interface](Interface): Menus, the HUD and the field of view
- [Engine](Engine): The engine's own settings, and the values it reports
- [Editing](Editing): Editing maps: entities, the map's settings, its lighting and sky, textures and materials
- [Scripting](Scripting): Binding keys, aliases, and the CubeScript commands that scripts are made of
- [Weapons](Weapons): Every weapon's settings, one set for each weapon named by its prefix, such as pistolammoclip
