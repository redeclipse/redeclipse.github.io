### Description

The Eclipse Rod is a super weapon announced upon map spawn (`modweight 8`, `modspeed -10`). Equipped with a laser sight and `99` rounds per clip (`198` reserve capacity), it dominates sightlines with cursor-guided dark beams and a devastating explosive secondary blast.

### Primary

Primary fire (`55` ms delay, full-auto, `1` ammo per shot) rapid-fires cursor-guided dark beams (`guided 1`, `speed 700`, spread `25`) that follow your aim in flight, pierce directly through players (`DRILL_PLAYER`), deal `9.5` base head damage (`95` internal), and spawn splitting Eclipse fragments on impact or flight.

### Secondary

Secondary fire (`1500` ms delay/charge time, consumes `33` ammo) charges up to unleash a high-speed antiparticle beam (`speed 10000`) with strong recoil (`kickpush 200`). On impact it detonates over a wide radius (`radial 60`, `10` base blast damage) and inflicts the [Burn](Status-Effects) residual effect on caught players.

### Tips

- Sweep your crosshair while firing primary bursts to steer dark beams around cover onto moving targets.
- Keep watch on ammo consumption with secondary fire: each blast spends a third of your `99`-round magazine (`33` ammo).
- Use the wide secondary blast on contested objectives, flag stands, or clustered enemies.

### Counterplay

Break line of sight immediately if you see the Eclipse Rod's laser sight or hear its spawn announcement, and push aggressively with close-range weapons while their secondary blast is on cooldown.

### Strengths & Weaknesses

**Strong against:** <br/>Open arenas, clustered defenders, and enemies dodging around corners.

**Weak against:** <br/>Close-range ambushes and sudden high-burst weapons.
