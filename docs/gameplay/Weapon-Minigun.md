### Description

The Heavy Minigun is one of Red Eclipse's four super weapons, announced on spawn and found at dedicated map locations. Despite a modest weight value (`modweight 3`), it carries the sharpest movement speed penalty in the game (`modspeed -50`), turning you into a slow-moving turret whenever it is drawn. It holds `500` rounds per clip and spawns with `1500` rounds (`ammospawn 1500`, `ammostore 0`).

### Primary

Primary fire unleashes a blistering stream of twin projectiles (`2` rays every `60` ms, spread `11`, speed `2700`) with heavy muzzle climb and damage that tapers down over flight distance. Shots bounce off surfaces and pierce shallow geometry (`drill 2`), shredding exposed opponents at close-to-medium range.

### Secondary

Secondary fire (`500` ms delay, consumes `4` ammo per shot) launches a large bouncing sphere of shrapnel that explodes on impact into `25` SMG secondary-style shrapnel fragments, ideal for clearing corridors or finishing targets around corners.

### Tips

- Switch to your Pistol or other loadout weapon while rotating across the map so the `-50` speed penalty does not leave you vulnerable.
- Compensate for heavy vertical recoil on sustained primary bursts, or feather the trigger at medium range.
- Bounce secondary shrapnel orbs into enclosed rooms before pushing in.

### Counterplay

Maintain long sightlines or flank around cover. Because the Heavy Minigun carrier moves slowly while firing it, Laser Rifles, Zappers, and long-range explosives can punish them before they close distance.

### Strengths & Weaknesses

**Strong against:** <br/>Grouped opponents and close-to-medium range corridors.

**Weak against:** <br/>Long-range precision weapons and mobile ambushes catching you off guard while slowed.
